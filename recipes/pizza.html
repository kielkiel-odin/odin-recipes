Pizza

Description of Pizza:

Pizza is one of the most iconic Italian dishes, known and enjoyed all over the world. It consists of a flat, round dough topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and a variety of toppings such as vegetables, meats, or herbs, then baked to perfection in an oven. The classic Neapolitan pizza, with its thin, soft crust and fresh mozzarella, is a cultural treasure, while countless regional and modern variations exist globally. Pizza is versatile—ranging from a quick street food to a gourmet dish—and remains a universal symbol of comfort and sharing.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough (flour, yeast, salt, water, olive oil)
  • Tomato sauce
  • Olive oil
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Optional toppings (pepperoni, mushrooms, vegetables, etc.)

Steps on how to cook it:

  1. Prepare or buy pizza dough and let it rise.
  2. Preheat the oven (preferably with a pizza stone).
  3. Roll out the dough into a thin round base.
  4. Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  5. Drizzle with olive oil and add fresh basil.
  6. Bake until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling.
  7. Slice and serve hot.
