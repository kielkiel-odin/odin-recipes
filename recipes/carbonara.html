Carbonara

Description of Carbonara

Carbonara is a traditional Italian pasta dish that originated in Rome. It is made with simple yet flavorful ingredients—typically spaghetti, eggs, Pecorino Romano cheese, guanciale (cured pork cheek), and freshly ground black pepper. The sauce is creamy but contains no cream; instead, it comes from the emulsion of eggs and cheese mixed with the hot pasta. Carbonara is loved for its rich, savory taste and silky texture, striking a perfect balance between indulgence and simplicity.

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti (or another long pasta)
  • Guanciale (or pancetta as an alternative)
  • Eggs (usually just the yolks or a mix of yolks and whole eggs)
  • Pecorino Romano cheese
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • Salt (for pasta water)

Steps on how to cook it:

  1. Boil pasta in salted water until al dente.
  2. While pasta cooks, sauté guanciale until crispy.
  3. Drain pasta, reserving some cooking water.
  4. Toss hot pasta with guanciale, then remove from heat.
  5. Mix in egg and cheese mixture, adding reserved pasta water to create a creamy sauce.
  6. Serve immediately with extra cheese and pepper on top.
